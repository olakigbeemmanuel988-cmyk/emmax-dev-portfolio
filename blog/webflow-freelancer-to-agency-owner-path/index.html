The Lantern of Absence

It offered no light, yet it drew every shadow toward it, as though absence itself radiated with a pull greater than flame. Its glass held nothing, its frame carried no warmth, yet around it gathered reverence, as if those who passed knew they were in the presence of something holy. The lantern did not illuminate the path—it illuminated the impossibility of illumination, reminding all who looked upon it that darkness was not the absence of light but the presence of something else entirely. In its emptiness, it burned brighter than any flame, casting not visibility, but awareness of the space beyond reach.


Conclusion

Even without flame, the lantern guides attention through emptiness. Its presence reminds us that illumination is not always literal, and that absence can reveal as much as light ever could.
